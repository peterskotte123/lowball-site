Average rent in San Marcos, CA

The typical asking rent in San Marcos is $3,495 a month across 77 whole-home listings seen on Oct 30, 2026. A 1-bed asks a median $2,245 and a 2-bed $2,995. Other sites quote different figures because they count different homes. This one counts every whole-home rental listing, houses included, and uses the typical (median) ask rather than the average, so a few luxury homes do not drag it up.
