Average rent in San Diego, CA

The typical asking rent in San Diego is $3,350 a month across 6,087 whole-home listings seen between Oct 20 and Oct 30, 2026. A 1-bed asks a median $2,100 and a 2-bed $2,900. Other sites quote different figures because they count different homes. This one counts every whole-home rental listing, houses included, and uses the typical (median) ask rather than the average, so a few luxury homes do not drag it up.
