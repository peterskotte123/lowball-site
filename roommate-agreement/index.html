What is a roommate agreement?

It is a written agreement among the people sharing a rental about how they will share it: who pays what, what happens to the deposit, how bills are divided, and what happens if someone leaves. It is separate from the lease. The lease is between the tenants and the landlord and says what the landlord is owed; the roommate agreement is among the tenants and says how they divide that between themselves. Most disputes between roommates are about money and moving out, so those are the parts to get right. The template above covers rent, the deposit, utilities, early move-out, guests and shared costs in plain language, and it takes a few minutes to fill in.

Is a roommate agreement legally binding?

It can be. A written agreement that the roommates sign is a contract among them, and the money terms, such as who owes what share of the rent, the bills and the deposit, are the kind of terms a small claims court can enforce. House rules about chores or noise are harder to enforce and work mostly because everyone agreed to them. Two limits matter. The agreement cannot change the lease: if the lease makes every tenant responsible for the whole rent, the landlord can still ask any one of you for all of it, and the agreement only gives you a claim against the roommate who did not pay. And it cannot allow what the lease or the law forbids, such as an unapproved sublet. This is general information, not legal advice.

What should a roommate agreement include?

Start with the money, because that is what goes wrong. Write down each person's share of the rent, the day it is due and who pays the landlord. Record what each person paid toward the security deposit and how deductions will be shared, so there is no argument a year later about whose damage it was. Say how utilities and internet are divided and how quickly bills are settled. Then cover leaving: how much notice a roommate gives, and that they keep paying until a replacement the landlord accepts takes over. After that, add what matters in your household, such as overnight guests, pets, parking, quiet hours and cleaning. Keep it short enough that everyone will read it before signing.

How should roommates divide the rent?

Equally if the rooms are alike, and by room if they are not. The usual methods are an equal split, a split by bedroom size, and a mix that divides half the rent equally and half by size, with a flat amount on top for a private bathroom or the parking space.
