Lowball

Early access

Lowball is a Chrome extension for renters. Open a rental listing and it shows what similar places ask, how many landlords in that position have already cut, what to offer, and the letter to send. It is not in the Chrome Web Store yet, so installing takes two minutes by hand. Desktop Chrome only.

Download Lowball (zip, 45 KB)

Install

  1. Unzip the download. You get a folder named store; keep it somewhere you won't delete.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. A welcome page opens. Read what sharing sends and pick either button. Then open any rental listing on zillow.com.

What to expect

San Diego County is fully covered. Elsewhere you'll see a rough range from public rent data until people in your area use it; with sharing on, your own browsing fills your area in within days.

When something looks wrong, use "Wrong? Flag it" in the panel, or email peterskotte@lowball.rent. That feedback is the whole point of early access.
