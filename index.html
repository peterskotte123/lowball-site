How it works

Every number comes with its evidence: nearby comps, days on market, price cuts, posted specials, and the listing site's own rent estimate shown for comparison. You see the reasoning, not just a figure.

1

Browse rentals like normal

Search results get a chip on each card: how much a month it asks above similar listings nearby, or at market, or under, with an opening number. Triage the whole page at a glance.

2

Open a listing

The panel opens with what happens next: days listed, the share of listings like it that have already cut, and how much longer it is likely to sit. Then the offer, what it saves over a year, and why. Big buildings get per-unit numbers and the posted specials called out.

3

Copy the offer

One click drafts a polite, evidence-backed note to the landlord with one number and the comps behind it. Paste it, send it, and negotiate from evidence.

Install

Lowball is a Chrome extension. It runs only on zillow.com and talks to one server, ours, to score listings.

What we collect: listings, not people

To score a listing, the extension sends that listing's facts to our server: price, beds, baths, size, location, days listed, price history. Nothing about you. If you opt in, the listings you view also help improve estimates for everyone. No names, emails, or searches, ever. Turn it off any time from the panel.
