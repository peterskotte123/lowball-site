How it works

Every number comes with its evidence: nearby comps, days on market, price cuts, posted specials, and Zillow's own estimate. You see the reasoning, not just a figure.

1

Browse Zillow like normal

Search results get a chip on each card: over by 12%, at market, or under market, with an opening number. Triage the whole page at a glance.

2

Open a listing

The panel shows fair rent, what to open with, where to expect to land, and why. Big buildings get per-unit numbers and the posted specials called out.

3

Copy the offer

One click writes a polite, evidence-backed note to the landlord with your number and your fallback. Paste it, send it, save real money.

Install

Lowball is a Chrome extension. It runs only on zillow.com and talks to one server, ours, to score listings.

What we collect: listings, not people

To score a listing, the extension sends that listing's facts to our server: price, beds, baths, size, location, days listed, price history. Nothing about you. If you opt in, the listings you view also help improve estimates for everyone. No names, emails, or searches, ever. Turn it off any time from the panel.
